## PM2.5 breakpoints (24-hour average, µg/m³)
Good 0–30, Satisfactory 31–60, Moderate 61–90, Poor 91–120, Very Poor 121–250, Severe above 250. PM2.5 is fine particulate matter, small enough to reach deep into the lungs.

## PM10 breakpoints (24-hour average, µg/m³)
Good 0–50, Satisfactory 51–100, Moderate 101–250, Poor 251–350, Very Poor 351–430, Severe above 430.

## NO2 breakpoints (24-hour average, µg/m³)
Nitrogen dioxide: Good 0–40, Satisfactory 41–80, Moderate 81–180, Poor 181–280, Very Poor 281–400, Severe above 400. Mostly from vehicles and combustion.

## Ozone (O3) breakpoints (8-hour average, µg/m³)
Good 0–50, Satisfactory 51–100, Moderate 101–168, Poor 169–208, Very Poor 209–748, Severe above 748. Ground-level ozone forms in sunlight, so it peaks in the afternoon.

## CO breakpoints (8-hour average, mg/m³)
Carbon monoxide: Good 0–1.0, Satisfactory 1.1–2.0, Moderate 2.1–10, Poor 10–17, Very Poor 17–34, Severe above 34. Note the unit is milligrams, not micrograms: 2,000 µg/m³ equals 2 mg/m³.

## SO2 breakpoints (24-hour average, µg/m³)
Sulphur dioxide: Good 0–40, Satisfactory 41–80, Moderate 81–380, Poor 381–800, Very Poor 801–1600, Severe above 1600.

## NH3 breakpoints (24-hour average, µg/m³)
Ammonia: Good 0–200, Satisfactory 201–400, Moderate 401–800, Poor 801–1200, Very Poor 1201–1800, Severe above 1800.
