## Do air purifiers help with indoor pollution
Portable air cleaners (air purifiers) can reduce fine particles indoors, including PM2.5, the particles of greatest health concern. Many use a HEPA (high-efficiency particulate air) filter. No air cleaner removes every pollutant, so they work best together with keeping pollution sources out and ventilating when outdoor air is cleaner.

## How to choose an air purifier for a room
Instead of recommending brands, the EPA advises checking the Clean Air Delivery Rate (CADR), which measures how quickly a unit cleans particles from the air. A higher CADR suits a bigger room; for example, a 300 square foot room needs a CADR of at least 195 cubic feet per minute. Filters must be replaced regularly, because a dirty, overloaded filter stops working well.

## Ozone-generating air purifiers
Avoid air cleaners that produce ozone. Ozone is a lung irritant, and some technologies, such as ionizers and electrostatic precipitators, can give off ozone as a by-product.
