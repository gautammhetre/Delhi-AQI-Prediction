## Which months have the worst air in Delhi
In this dataset PM2.5 is highest from October to February. The monthly averages are roughly 250 µg/m³ in October, 377 in November, 381 in December, 342 in January and 425 in February. They are lowest in the monsoon, around 105 µg/m³ in July and 112 in August. Winter is worse because cold, still air traps pollution near the ground, and because of crop-residue (stubble) burning in neighbouring states in October and November.

## What time of day pollution is worst
On average, PM2.5 is highest late in the evening and at night, peaking around 10:30 pm, and lowest in the early afternoon around 2:30 pm. In the afternoon the sun heats the ground, the air mixes higher, and pollution spreads out. At night the air near the ground cools and stays put, trapping pollution. This is why the health advisory recommends avoiding early morning and late evening outdoor exercise.

## Diwali and air quality in the data
Diwali 2021 (4 November) had a daily average PM2.5 of about 607 µg/m³ against a November average of about 430. Diwali 2022 (24 October) was much cleaner, about 178 µg/m³, below that October's average of about 281, so the effect of firecrackers depends heavily on the weather that week.

## How often Delhi's air was severe
Using the NAQI PM2.5 bands on hourly values, about 33% of hours in the dataset fall in the Severe band and another 29% in Very Poor. Only about 3% of hours are Good. The average PM2.5 across the whole period is about 238 µg/m³.
