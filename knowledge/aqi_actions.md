## What to do when the AQI is Good or Satisfactory
At Good AQI (0 to 50) no special precautions are needed for anyone. At Satisfactory AQI (51 to 100) the general public needs no special precautions, while vulnerable people (young children, older adults, pregnant women and people with lung or heart disease) should do less prolonged or strenuous outdoor exertion.

## What to do when the AQI is Moderate
At Moderate AQI (101 to 200) the general public should do less prolonged or strenuous outdoor physical exertion, and vulnerable people should avoid prolonged or strenuous outdoor exertion altogether.

## What to do when the AQI is Poor
At Poor AQI (201 to 300) the general public should avoid outdoor physical exertion, and vulnerable people should avoid outdoor physical activities. This is also the level at which Delhi-NCR's GRAP Stage I measures apply.

## What to do when the AQI is Very Poor
At Very Poor AQI (301 to 400) the general public should avoid outdoor physical activities, especially in the early morning and late evening. Vulnerable people should remain indoors and keep activity levels low. GRAP Stage II applies in Delhi-NCR.

## What to do when the AQI is Severe
At Severe AQI (401 to 500) everyone should avoid outdoor physical activities, and vulnerable people should remain indoors and keep activity levels low. GRAP Stage III (AQI 401 to 450) or Stage IV (above 450) applies in Delhi-NCR.
