About Delhi AQI

Live air quality for any place in India, explained in plain language, with what to do about it.

Where the numbers come from

  • Live readings: Open-Meteo Air Quality (Copernicus CAMS model). These are modelled values for your area, not a ground monitoring station, so they can differ from CPCB station readings.
  • AQI: India's National Air Quality Index (CPCB). Particles, NO₂ and SO₂ use 24-hour averages; ozone and CO use 8-hour averages. The AQI is the highest pollutant score.
  • What to do: the Health Ministry's air-pollution advisory and CAQM's GRAP for Delhi-NCR.
  • Check your readings: a machine-learning model estimates PM2.5 from other pollutants{% if m %}.   • Delhi trends: hourly readings for Delhi from November 2020 to January 2023.

Privacy

  • "Use my location" asks your browser for permission. Your coordinates are rounded to about 1 km and used only to fetch readings. They're never put in a web address or saved to our database; the server keeps the readings for that rounded spot in memory for up to 10 minutes so repeat visits are fast.
  • Your last place, theme and "who is this for" choice are remembered in your own browser only.
  • Readings you enter on Check are saved without any personal details, so we can review how the tool is used. They're not shown to other visitors.

Limits

  • This is general guidance, not medical advice. If you have symptoms, see a doctor.
  • The estimate model was trained on Delhi data, so it's less reliable elsewhere.
  • GRAP rules change from time to time; CAQM's website has the version in force.
